Events in Historic Downtown Sanford

Want to know what’s really happening in Historic Downtown Sanford? 👀🎉

Our Facebook Group is the main hub for local events, with dozens of posts shared weekly directly by the bars, restaurants, venues, and event promoters themselves.

Live music, food and drink specials, festivals, pop-up events, and last-minute happenings all in one place. If it’s going on downtown, it’s probably posted here first.

👉 Join the group and stay in the loop: Downtown Sanford Events & Things To Do

Historic Downtown Sanford in Central Florida just 20 minutes North of Orlando, is a global food & drink destination with the most spectacular events in the region! From craft beer festivals, to music festivals, block parties, fundraisers, food truck rallies, indie markets, art festivals, parades, fireworks shows and lots lots more. Follow our socials on Instagram and Facebook and this Facebook Group Events to stay up to date on them all!
