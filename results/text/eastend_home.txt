 Hello Orlando! 
 
 Welcome To East End 
 
 
 
 East End Market is a neighborhood market and food hall in the Audubon Park Garden District of Orlando, Florida. The Market showcases some of Central Florida’s top food entrepreneurs, makers, artisans & chefs. The two-story structure is home to a dozen merchants, a large event space, a demonstration kitchen, an incubator kitchen, offices, retail shops, a full-time, award-winning caterer & a world-class ramen restaurant.

 “Have you had a chance to visit the East End Market yet? The food and shops are a joy but the real treat for me was the event space! It’s a unique venue. There are an outdoor courtyard and conference rooms. But the best spaces are the Audubon Park Exchange and the Boardroom. The spaces have wonderful open exposed ceilings with modern chandeliers. I’m in love with the brown folding chairs and how well it all goes together. This is a wonderful venue for intimate weddings but especially for rehearsal dinners, bridal showers and other intimate parties.”

 “The East End Market in Audubon Park is such a gem. They have the Domu restaurant, outside seating and various shops inside in the air conditioning. Some of the shops inside include gourmet cheese at La Femme du Fromage, bread at Olde Hearth Bread and coffee at Lineage Coffee.
You can get plants at Porch Therapy and homemade goods at Freehand Goods. There are so many choices in one location. I ran into my coworker leaving with cookies from Gideon's Bakehouse. Love the huge cookies there!”

 “This is for Domu! Domu made one of the best meals I've had in my life! Their food has only increased in quality since they opened. I had an Old Fashioned with Japanese whiskey to start off with- beautifully crafted with just the right amount of punch. Next we had the kimchi (I'm Korean and it's honestly some of the best kimchi I've ever had), and the potato appetizer which had a perfect combination of spice and sauce. I had the Tokyo ramen which had a rich broth and flavorful chicken- a perfect combination of flavor and texture. We finished our meal with their soft serve of the week- Vietnamese iced coffee. It was the perfect balance of sweet cream and strong coffee. I will definitely be back. It's also a great value for the quality of food that you get!”
