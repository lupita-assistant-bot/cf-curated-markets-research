 The Gem of the Hills Awaits You!
 Explore downtown delights, community events, and timeless small-town hospitality.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Clermont is a true gem 
 . And we believe its brightest days are still ahead. 
 With miles of scenic lakefront, rich history, and a growing community of passionate locals and entrepreneurs, Clermont has all the ingredients for a thriving downtown. We are Clermont Main Street—a volunteer-powered, nonprofit organization dedicated to revitalizing and celebrating the heart of our city through place-based economic development and community connection.

 Who We Are 
 
 
 
 We invite you to learn about our mission to bring Clermont together through active community engagement that revitalizes our historic downtown, celebrates our history, fosters innovation and creativity, and grows our local economy for the benefit and enjoyment of residents and visitors alike.

 Get involved. 
 
 
 
 Join us in revitalizing Clermont through volunteer opportunities that make a difference. Whether it's public art, historic preservation, innovative events, or legacy projects, we have a variety of initiatives for everyone to enjoy. 
 As a nonprofit organization, Clermont Main Street relies on your support to enhance our beloved town for this generation and future ones. Your involvement can help create a better Clermont for all.
